# Brainstorming workbench — after the pillar-course playtest

## Assessment priority

The user prioritises an exceptional creative brainstorming process: broad innovative exploration, evidence of originality, and deliberate use of brainstorming techniques. A functioning waterbending prototype is our test instrument, not sufficient evidence of a distinctive final concept. Avatar is an acknowledged reference, not our originality claim.

The SCAMPER session has not been completed. Do not present these prompts or empty logs as completed research, generated ideas, or playtest findings.

## Evidence already available

- User feedback: the first elastic blob felt like molten glass. We changed to sourced low-poly streams.
- User decision: released water stays where it lands and can be bent again; sources replenish.
- Play feedback drove smoother ice, a usable jump, midair steering, and more forgiving slopes.
- The updated vision changed freezing from a global toggle to independent route constructions.
- The pillar course tests the proposition: the destination is known, but the route is invented.

These are recorded iterations and design decisions. They do not establish that the final idea is unique or that a full reference-game analysis has been done.

## Session procedure

1. Capture the user's itch.io analysis and pillar-course observations. Separate observations, interpretations, and unanswered questions.
2. Diverge before judging: aim for 4-6 different ideas per SCAMPER operation (28-42 seeds), varying more than elemental powers.
3. Explore source/material, player role, environment, route purpose, time, cost, and relationships. Include surprising ideas and alternatives that could fail.
4. Cluster recurring themes, then combine seeds across different operations. Build three substantially different concept directions.
5. Evaluate the directions for expressive traversal, distinctive decisions, fantasy/role, readability, feasibility, and testability. Compare specific overlaps with references; avoid unsupported claims of global uniqueness.
6. Record rejected alternatives and reasons. Choose one small experiment, not an entire new feature list.

## SCAMPER prompts

| Operation | Exploration prompt |
| --- | --- |
| Substitute | What could replace the material, source, protagonist role, or route-building purpose while preserving physical sculpting? |
| Combine | What unrelated activity or system would make water/ice construction create a new decision rather than just another power? |
| Adapt | What processes from engineering, ecology, craft, transport, or social cooperation could change how a route is made or used? |
| Modify / magnify / minimise | What changes if scale, persistence, visibility, time, volume, or responsibility is exaggerated or reduced? |
| Put to another use | Who or what besides the player might need the route, and how would that change the shapes worth making? |
| Eliminate | Which familiar elemental-power assumptions could disappear? What remains interesting without combat, unlimited supply, or permanent construction? |
| Reverse / rearrange | What changes if water controls the player, construction is undone rather than added, the destination moves, or the consequences occur in another order? |

## Logs to fill during the session

Idea ledger: seed ID; SCAMPER operation; inspiration/observation; specific player action; consequence; novelty argument; tradeoff; possible combination; status.

Concept comparison: fantasy/role; core decision loop; two or three combined seeds; distinction from named references; what could be fun; what could fail; smallest falsifiable prototype.

Playtest observation log: situation; player intention; actual outcome; felt response (user-reported); question raised; candidate SCAMPER operation. Leave findings unfilled until observed or supplied by the user.

## Banked experiments

Slippery ice; encapsulating objects; pulling/pushing objects with liquid; pushing things onto/off committed ice; ledge climbing onto constructed ledges. These are seeds to examine, not an approved implementation queue or proof of originality. Independent ice and liquid masses are already implemented.
